LICH SU XAC NHAN      http://127.0.0.1:8000/nhan-vien/lich-su/

Giao dien mau xanh la - kem, font Be Vietnam Pro, su dung anh
static/images/duyen-banner.jpg va logo static/images/duyen-logo.svg
DA CO trong website Duyen Spa cua ban.

CHUC NANG
- Nhan vien xem tat ca lich, loc ngay, trang thai, tim kiem, xem theo gio.
- Nhan vien chi duoc "Xac nhan den" (chuyen thang sang Da hoan thanh)
  hoac "Khong den" (sau gio hen).
- Lich su xac nhan chi hien thi cac thao tac do CHINH tai khoan do lam.
- Chu spa giu nguyen Dashboard, nguoi dat lich giu nguyen trang khach.
- Nhan vien khong tao, sua, huy lich hay quan ly dich vu.

YEU CAU
- Da cai va migrate Admin Dashboard V6.
- Da co tai khoan nhan vien is_staff=True, is_superuser=False.
- Da co static/images/duyen-banner.jpg va duyen-logo.svg.

CAC BUOC
1. DUNG server Django trong PyCharm: Ctrl+C.
2. SAO LUU ca thu muc du an va db.sqlite3; khong sua ban backup.
3. Giai nen file ZIP, copy tat ca thu muc crmg2, static va file
   cai_dat_nhan_vien_duyen_spa.py vao thu muc co manage.py.
4. Trong Terminal (tai thu muc co manage.py), chay:
      py cai_dat_nhan_vien_duyen_spa.py
5. Neu bao "THANH CONG", chay:
      py manage.py check
      py manage.py runserver
6. Mo cua so an danh, dang nhap nhanvien01 tai /dang-nhap/.
   He thong dua vao /nhan-vien/lich-hen/.
7. Test bang MOT lich HEN THU (khong xu ly nham lich cua khach that).

QUAN TRONG
- NEU bao "DUNG CAI DAT", KHONG chay lenh lan hai lan; gui loi.
- KHONG chay makemigrations/migrate: bo nay khong sua model hay DB.
- Khong sao chep trang HTML demo vao templates: demo chi co du lieu gia.
- Trang web khach hang va Dashboard chu spa khong bi thay layout.
- Trinh cai se luu code cu vao _backup_nhan_vien_<ngay_gio>.
- Neu banner khong hien, kiem tra file static/images/duyen-banner.jpg.
